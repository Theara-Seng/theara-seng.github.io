## 🏆 Overview
In February 2026, our team was awarded Second Prize at the National Youth E-GEN Innovation Competition organized by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS), Cambodia. The competition focused on developing innovative IoT, Machine Learning, and AI-based solutions to address real-world community challenges.

We presented our project during the final round at the Ministry headquarters in Phnom Penh
